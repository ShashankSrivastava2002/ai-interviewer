Tell me about a hard technical problem you solved there.

You
04:10
The hardest was our appointment-booking pipeline. Customers email bookings as PDFs, Excel or free text, sometimes six hundred appointments in one email. My first version used a single agent loop, and it timed out at the API gateway after ten minutes and burned thirty thousand tokens per request. I redesigned it as a DAG: one node normalises the document, one LLM node extracts appointment ID, date and slot into a strict JSON schema, and a code node books them in parallel. I chose a DAG over a multi-agent setup because the steps are fixed and I needed predictable latency and cost. Latency dropped from about ten minutes to one, tokens fell from thirty thousand to under ten thousand, and that cut our monthly LLM bill for that pipeline by roughly sixty percent.

You
